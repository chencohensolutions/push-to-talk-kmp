# Your picture

Settings › Account › Your picture, and once after enrolling (or after updating to the version with pictures), before
home. See docs/design.md, "Pictures".

- **Before a photo:** the current picture (or a letter circle), a line on what it is, **Take a selfie** (the camera app,
  asked to start on the front camera) and **Pick a photo** (the photo picker), and the privacy line: drawn on this phone,
  the photo never kept or sent. Onboarding adds **Skip for now**; it never blocks, with or without a network.
- **After a photo:** "Pick the one you like" over a 2×2 grid, one tile per style (Soft, Painted, Hand-drawn, Ink). Each
  tile is split light and dark down the middle, so the cut-out shows on both themes. A tile shows a progress ring while
  its model downloads, a spinner while it draws, or "Needs the internet once". The first drawn is chosen; a tap chooses
  another (thick ink border).
- **Use this picture** saves it and sends it to contacts and groups, end to end. Take a selfie and Pick a photo stay
  below to try again.
- No face in the photo: a red line asks for one with the face clear and facing the camera.
- From Settings, with a picture: **Remove my picture** (red text).
- The picture shows free-standing, with no disc and no circle crop, wherever a letter circle was. The reader circle
  becomes the face at 26 dp.
