UI icons with the app icon's detail

The app icon is built from a front shape, a softer shape behind it, a thin light edge and one teal part. The small icons take the first three as opacities of a single tint, so every icon still takes one colour from where it is used, exactly as today. A second colour is the exception: six icons, and only where the colour means something.

  • Line. Full strength, 1.75 wide (2 today), round ends, corners 2.5. Today's shapes on today's 24-unit grid, a little rounder.
  • Soft fill. Every closed body is filled with the same tint at 8% (10% in dark): a hint of a second tone, never a grey shape.
  • Solid part and inner edge. One small part may be solid (the mic's head, the camera's lens, the bell's clapper, the server's lights), and a 1-unit line at 30% runs inside the body, top left. It reads at 32 and up and fades by itself at 24 and 18.
  • Off. A crossed-out icon has no solid part, and a gap around its bar.
  • Second colour, six icons. The solid part alone takes it: teal while live (Mic, Video, Talk, Call), coral for an urgent push (Bell), red for removing (Trash). Everywhere else these six are one tint like the rest.
  • Plain ones stay plain. Arrows, chevrons, Close, Plus, Check, Expand and Flip have no body to fill: only their weight changes.

The full set, 48 px, one tint

Each icon is one colour with three strengths. Rows: live, people and chat, trust and system, plain. Error and CloudOff are shown in the colours the app gives them (red, the warning orange) to show the tint is still the screen's choice.
