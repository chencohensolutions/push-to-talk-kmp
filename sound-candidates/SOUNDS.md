# Message sound candidates: real wind chimes

Recorded wind chimes, each edited into a calm ~2.3 s notification:
- one gentle cluster of chimes;
- the wind's rumble cut (high-pass at 350 Hz);
- a 30 ms fade in and a 0.9 s fade out;
- mono, 48 kHz, normalized to about -20 LUFS (quiet).

Each comes as `.ogg` (Opus 64 kbit/s, for the app) and `.mp3` (for listening). Made 2026-11-23 for hen's pick.
